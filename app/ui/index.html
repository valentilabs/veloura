      Help

      Quick start

      1. Describe a vibe in Studio.
      2. Click Add to FL. Real, editable notes land in FL.
      3. Ask for changes: “darker”, “less busy”, “add hats”.

      Best tip

      Best way to get a melody: load the sound in FL first, then tell Veloura what to play on it.

      “add slow string chords in ZENOLOGY 2”

      Use Veloura with a paid ChatGPT plan

      ChatGPT is the recommended way to use Veloura. It needs a paid plan; a free ChatGPT account won't connect.

      • ChatGPT GoCheapest, lowest limits. Not in every country.
      • ChatGPT Plus$20/month. Plenty for most producers.
      • ChatGPT ProHighest limits.

      Other options: a Google Gemini key (free tier), a Claude key (pay per use), or Offline (free, not AI).

      Questions

      Veloura can't connect to FL Studio
      Do these in order:
      “FL didn't respond”
      FL was probably restarted. Open any Piano Roll and choose Tools ▸ Scripts ▸ veloura_bridge once. Close any open dialog in FL.
      The controller won't turn green
      Open Veloura before FL. If FL is already open, click Restart FL for me on the red banner in Studio.
      Accessibility keeps asking
      System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility: turn on Veloura, then quit and reopen Veloura.
      A part went to the wrong channel
      Pick the right channel from the part's menu. Veloura moves it and remembers your choice.
      Drums went to the Piano Roll
      The FL controller isn't connected, or no channel name matches. See Setup, step 3.
      Undo something
      Press Cmd+Z in FL, or click Undo on a part. To reverse everything: click the project name ▸ Undo everything Veloura did.
      The AI says it's busy
      Wait a minute and try again, or switch AI in Settings.
      What Veloura can't do yet
      Insert effects, arrange the Playlist or hear your beat. It can pick presets, program drums, write melodies, set the tempo and mix, and gives short steps for the rest.
