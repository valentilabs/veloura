Install

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Veloura into Applications.
  2. Open Veloura. It walks you through the MIDI ports and the one FL script it needs.
  3. Continue with ChatGPT, or add a Gemini or Claude key.

If it won't connect to FL

  1. Quit FL Studio and Veloura.
  2. Open Veloura first, then FL Studio.
  3. Open your project and go straight to the Piano Roll.
  4. Link the bridge: Tools › Scripts › veloura_bridge.
FAQ

Questions

Is Veloura Beta really free?

Yes. The download is free and donations are optional. AI use counts toward your paid ChatGPT plan, runs on Gemini's free tier, or costs from about $0.004 a beat with a Claude key.

Is the beta the real Veloura?

No. Veloura Beta is a free early version. The official Veloura is a separate paid app that comes later, and beta users hear about it first inside the app. There's no price or date yet.

Does it work with free ChatGPT?

No. OpenAI refuses free accounts, so ChatGPT sign-in needs Go, Plus or Pro. For a free option, use a Gemini API key on Google's free tier (not available in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), or the offline composer.

Which ChatGPT plan should I get?

Any paid plan works. Ranked worst to best for Veloura: Go (cheapest, lowest limits), Plus ($20 a month, the one we test on), Business, Enterprise or Edu (not tested yet), and Pro (highest limits). Bigger plans let you make more beats before hitting limits.

Does it make audio or MIDI?

MIDI. Veloura writes notes into the Piano Roll and steps into the Channel Rack, played by sounds and kits you already have.

Can I release what I make?

Veloura claims no ownership of what you make. Clearing any samples you use is up to you, and your AI provider's terms also apply.

Windows or Intel Macs?

Not yet. The beta runs on Macs with Apple silicon.

Is Veloura Beta made by Image-Line?

No. Veloura Beta is an independent app that works with FL Studio through FL's scripting features. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Image-Line.

Where do donations go?

Straight to the solo developer behind Veloura, through a third-party payment page. Donations are optional, don't unlock anything, and keep the free beta updated.
